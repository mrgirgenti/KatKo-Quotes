# Reports Page Redesign — Replace Current Version

The current Reports page is functional but does not match the dashboard design we previously approved.

The biggest issue is that the page relies heavily on tables. Reports should function as an executive dashboard, not a spreadsheet.

Do not make minor tweaks to the existing layout. Rebuild the Overview tab to match the structure below while preserving existing data calculations, filtering logic, and date range functionality.

Use the attached mockup as the visual reference for layout proportions, spacing, hierarchy, and sizing.

---

# Objective

The Reports page should quickly answer:

1. How is the business performing?
2. What is generating revenue?
3. What is generating profit?
4. What needs attention?
5. Where should I focus next?

The page should feel visual, actionable, and operational.

# Top KPI Section

This is the most important area of the page.

Display exactly five KPI cards:

* Revenue
* Profit
* Orders
* Avg Order
* Avg PCS

Requirements:

* 5 cards across
* Equal widths
* Equal heights
* Minimum card height: 120px
* Card padding: 22–24px
* Gap between cards: 16px
* Same styling as other KPI cards throughout Katalyst OS

Value styling:

* Large and visually dominant
* Bold
* Primary focus of card

Label styling:

* Small uppercase or semibold
* Consistent across all KPI cards

Do not allow KPI cards to compress or wrap.

No additional KPI cards.
