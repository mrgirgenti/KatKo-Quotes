Client Hub Activation + Hub Management Stabilization

This is a bug-fix and architecture cleanup task.

The current Client Hub activation flow is not functioning correctly and must be audited end-to-end.

Execute all items below.

---

## PROBLEM SUMMARY

Client Hub activation is unreliable and appears disconnected from the Organization being managed.

Current behavior:

1. Contacts already exist inside the Organization.

2. Hub Settings does not properly surface those contacts.

3. Enabling Client Hub access on a Contact does not appear to create or assign Hub access.

4. Hub On / Hub Off state does not persist correctly.

5. Hub Readiness activates visually but actual Hub activation does not remain saved.

6. Org Admin and Team Member management are fragmented and disconnected from Organization Contacts.

The entire Hub Management workflow needs to be audited and stabilized.
