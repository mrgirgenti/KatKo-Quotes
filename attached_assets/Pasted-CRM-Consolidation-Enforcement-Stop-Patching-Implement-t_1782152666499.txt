## BACKGROUND

Over multiple implementation phases, the intended architecture was established:

Organization
↓
Contacts
↓
Client Hub

Contacts are the source of truth.

However, implementation has continued to preserve legacy member-management concepts alongside Contacts.

This has resulted in:

* Contacts showing one thing
* Hub Management showing another
* Duplicate counts
* Duplicate management interfaces
* Synchronization bugs
* Repeated fixes that do not solve the underlying issue

Example:

Organization Details:
Contacts = 2

Hub Management:
Members = 0
"No contacts yet"

This is a failure of architecture, not a UI bug.
