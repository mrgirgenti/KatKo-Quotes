Important Rules

Catalogs are supplier relationships.

Products are curated recommendations.

Catalogs are NOT Products.

Products are NOT Catalogs.

Catalog APIs must never automatically create Product records.

Catalog Products remain separate from Recommended Products.

Only deliberate user actions should create Recommended Products.
