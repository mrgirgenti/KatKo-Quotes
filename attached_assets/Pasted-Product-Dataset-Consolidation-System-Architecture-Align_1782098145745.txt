Product Dataset Consolidation + System Architecture Alignment

STOP.

Before performing any product migration, deletion, reset, import, quote integration, mockup integration, or catalog consolidation, align the architecture around the following requirements.

This is the approved system direction.

Source of Truth

The curated catalog spreadsheet is the source of truth.

Products represents Katalyst Ko's recommended products.

Products does NOT represent every product we can sell.

Products does NOT represent every product available from vendor catalogs.

Products is a curated library.

The user has finalized the product list.

Do not add products.

Do not remove products.

Do not replace products.

Do not optimize products.

Do not recommend alternative products.

Do not import products that are not included in the approved catalog spreadsheet.

Architecture Goal

The Products database is not just a page.

The Products database is a shared Product Dataset.

The following systems must all consume the same Product Dataset:

* Products Page
* New Quote → Products + Sizes
* Start A Project → Products + Sizes
* Mockup Designer
* Client Hubs

Do not maintain separate product lists.

Do not maintain separate color lists.

Do not maintain separate vendor lists.

Do not maintain separate garment style lists.

All product information should originate from the Product Dataset.

Product Dataset Responsibilities

The Product Dataset becomes the source of truth for:

* Style Number
* Brand
* Product Name
* Category
* Product Type
* Colors
* Vendors
* Placement Template
* Default Cost
* Assets

Products Page

The Products page is only an interface for managing the Product Dataset.

It is not the source of truth.

The Product Dataset is the source of truth.

Catalog Strategy

Products are curated recommendations.

Future vendor catalogs remain separate.

Examples:

* S&S Activewear
* SanMar
* McCreary's
* Shaka Wear
* Independent Trading
* LA Apparel
* Momentec

Future vendor APIs should NOT import thousands of products into Products.

Future workflow:

Priority 1:
Curated Products

Priority 2:
Vendor Catalog Search

Priority 3:
Manual Product Entry

Products should remain a curated recommendation library.

Quotes must never be restricted by Products.

Quotes

Quotes must support:

1. Curated Products
2. Manual Products
3. Future API Products

Products enhance quoting.

Products never restrict quoting.

If a Product is selected:

Store an optional productId reference.

Do not require productId.

Manual products must remain valid.

API products must remain valid.

Snapshot Rule

The Product record contains a Default Cost.

When a Product is selected from:

* Client Hub
* Start A Project
* New Quote

the Default Cost may be used to pre-populate the Quote line item Product Cost field.

After the Quote line item is created:

The Quote line item owns its own Product Cost value.

Changes made to the Quote line item cost do not update the Product record.

Future changes to the Product record do not update existing Quotes.

Product Cost provides the default value.

Quote Cost becomes the source of truth.

Track:

* default_product_cost
* manual_override

as the cost source.

Client Hub Workflow

Clients never see pricing.

Clients may select:

* Product
* Color
* Sizes

from the Product Dataset.

Submitted projects should preserve the selected Product reference.

When a team member converts a submitted project into a quote:

Auto-populate:

* Product
* Color
* Vendor options
* Default Cost

from the Product Dataset.

The default cost becomes the initial quote value.

The quote may then be edited independently.

Mockup Designer

The Mockup Designer should consume the same Product Dataset.

Do not maintain a separate mockup product catalog.

Product selection should come from the Product Dataset.

Colors should come from the Product Dataset.

Placement templates should come from the Product Dataset.

Mockups must still support:

* Curated Products
* Manual Products
* Generic Mockup Products

because not every quoted product will exist in the Product Dataset.

Migration Safety Rules

Before any migration:

1. Compare spreadsheet against current Product Dataset.

2. Produce:

* Products to Create
* Products to Update
* Products to Ignore

3. Do not delete anything automatically.

4. Do not migrate products not found in the approved spreadsheet.

5. Present migration results for approval before executing.

* Client Hub impact
* Mockup Designer impact
* Catalog impact
* Risks
* Recommended implementation order

Do not perform migration.
