REPORTS PAGE REDESIGN & RESTRUCTURE

OBJECTIVE

Redesign the Reports page to better match the Katalyst OS design language, improve usability on desktop and mobile, eliminate redundant information, and focus on actionable business intelligence rather than dashboard clutter.

The final Reports page should feel like the rest of Katalyst OS:

* Black section headers
* Orange action links/buttons
* Compact layout
* Mobile-friendly
* Consistent typography and spacing
* Business-focused metrics
* No oversized charts or wasted whitespace

---

## PAGE STRUCTURE

Add sub-navigation directly inside the Reports page (NOT in the sidebar).

Tabs:

* Overview
* Financial
* Customers
* Services

These should appear at the top of the Reports page similar to the tab structure used throughout the platform.

## OVERVIEW TAB

TOP KPI ROW

Use 5 KPI cards.

Each KPI should display:

* Current Value
* Percentage Change
* Comparison Period

KPIs:

1. Revenue
2. Profit
3. Orders
4. Avg Order
5. Avg PCS

Calculations:

Revenue
= Total Revenue

Profit
= Total Profit

Orders
= Total Orders

Avg Order
= Revenue ÷ Orders

Avg PCS
= Total PCS ÷ Orders

Example:

Revenue
$118,420
▲ 22.4%

Profit
$24,880
▲ 18.1%

Orders
147
▲ 11.3%

Avg Order
$806
▲ 9.8%

Avg PCS
42
▲ 15.2%

IMPORTANT:

Remove PCS from the primary metrics area elsewhere on the Reports page.

Avg PCS replaces PCS as the more meaningful business KPI.

## REMOVE

Remove:

* Revenue by Month chart
* Profit by Month chart
* Margin Over Time chart
* Revenue Trend chart
* Customer Growth chart
* Top Performing Projects section
* Any large visual charts that consume significant vertical space

Do NOT replace them with additional charts.

Keep Reports focused on actionable business data.

## DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Match existing Katalyst OS patterns:

* Black section headers
* White content cards
* Orange actions
* Consistent typography
* Consistent spacing
* Consistent border radius

Avoid oversized cards.

Reduce vertical whitespace wherever possible.

Design should work equally well on:

* Desktop
* Tablet
* Mobile

without requiring major layout changes.

Prioritize information density and usability over decorative analytics visuals.

The Reports page should feel like an operational business intelligence center, not a generic SaaS analytics dashboard.
