What you're looking at in that screenshot is effectively a **duplicate project management interface** that has evolved separately from the Organization Detail implementation you've been refining.

There should be two very different experiences:

### Global Projects (Sidebar → Projects)

This is your operational command center.

Purpose:

* View all projects across all clients
* Production queue management
* Search entire company workload
* Sort by Order Date
* Filter by status
* Filter by service
* Filter by salesperson
* Filter by production status
* Manage workflow

The screenshot you attached belongs here.

---

### Organization → Projects Tab

This is client-specific.

Purpose:

* Show only projects for that client
* Show only quotes for that client
* Quick review of history
* Quick access to project records
* Navigate into project details

This should use the card design you've been perfecting:
