====================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================

Implement a Quote-Level Discount feature.

This applies ONLY to the Quote Summary.

The discount should affect the ENTIRE quote.

Never an individual Line Item.

====================================================
LOCATION
====================================================

The Discount section belongs inside the Quote Summary.

Place it after the Quote Subtotal.

Before Tax.

Before Grand Total.

The flow should become:

Subtotal

↓

Discount

↓

Tax

↓

Grand Total

====================================================
DISCOUNT TYPES
====================================================

Support two discount types.

Percentage

Dollar Amount

User chooses via dropdown.

10%

15%

20%

$50

$100

$250

====================================================
CUSTOM VALUES
====================================================

Allow custom entry.

Users are NOT restricted to preset values.

====================================================
DISCOUNT BASIS
====================================================

The discount is applied against the Gross Quote Subtotal.

NOT Net.

NOT Product Cost.

NOT Service Cost.

NOT Profit.

Simply reduce the customer selling price.
