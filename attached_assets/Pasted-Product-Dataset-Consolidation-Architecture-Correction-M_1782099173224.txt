Architecture Correction

The approved curated product spreadsheet is now the authoritative source for the Product Dataset.

The spreadsheet is the source of truth.

vendorCatalog.ts is NOT the source of truth.

types/quote.ts is NOT the source of truth.

Legacy quote product lists are NOT the source of truth.

Legacy mockup product lists are NOT the source of truth.

These legacy systems should only be used as supplemental data sources for:

* Color palettes
* Hex color values
* Garment metadata
* Historical style information
* Migration assistance

They are not the authoritative catalog.

Catalogs

Catalogs

Catalogs represent supplier relationships.

Examples:

* S&S Activewear
* SanMar
* McCreary's
* Shaka Wear
* Independent Trading
* LA Apparel
* Momentec

Catalogs remain separate from Products.

Catalogs are responsible for:

* Catalog PDFs
* Website links
* Client Hub visibility
* Featured status
* Future API credentials
* Future product imports
* Supplier relationship management

Products

Products are Katalyst Ko Recommended Products.

Products are curated.

Products are not vendor catalogs.

Products are not every product we can sell.

Products are not every product available through APIs.

Products are the shared Product Dataset.

The Product Dataset becomes the source of truth for:

* Style Number
* Brand
* Product Name
* Category
* Product Type
* Colors
* Available Catalogs
* Placement Templates
* Assets
* Default Cost

Shared Product Dataset

The following systems should consume the same Product Dataset:

* Products Page
* New Quote → Products + Sizes
* Start A Project → Products + Sizes
* Client Hubs
* Mockup Designer

Separate product lists should not exist.

Separate color lists should not exist.

Separate garment style lists should not exist.

Separate curated product catalogs should not exist.

Recommended Products vs Catalog Products vs Manual Products

The system must support three distinct product sources.

1. Recommended Products (Priority 1)

These are curated Katalyst Ko products stored in the Product Dataset.

Examples:

* NL6210
* BC3001
* 1717
* 18500
* K500

These are the products Katalyst Ko wants clients and team members to choose first.

These products power:

* Products Page
* Client Hubs
* Quotes
* Projects
* Mockups

2. Catalog Products (Priority 2)

These originate from Catalogs and future vendor APIs.

Examples:

* S&S Activewear
* SanMar
* McCreary's
* Shaka Wear
* Independent Trading
* LA Apparel
* Momentec

Catalog Products are searchable.

Catalog Products are not automatically added to the Product Dataset.

Catalog Products remain separate from Recommended Products.

If a Catalog Product becomes commonly used:

Allow a deliberate action:

"Add to Recommended Products"

Only then should it become part of the Product Dataset.

3. Manual Products (Priority 3)

Users must always be able to manually enter:

* Style Number
* Brand
* Product Name

Manual Products do not require a Product record.

Manual Products do not require a Catalog record.

Manual Products remain valid throughout the system.

Future API Rule

Catalog APIs must never automatically create Product records.

Catalog APIs remain a searchable source.

This separation is intentional and must be preserved.

Migration Planning Requirements

Before any migration:

Step 1

Export all existing Product records.

Step 2

Compare:

* Existing Product records
* Approved spreadsheet

Step 3

Produce four buckets:

KEEP
CREATE
UPDATE
ARCHIVE

Definitions:

KEEP

Existing Product exists in spreadsheet and requires no change.

CREATE

Product exists in spreadsheet but does not exist in Product Dataset.

UPDATE

Product exists in both places but requires changes.

ARCHIVE

Existing Product does not exist in spreadsheet and should be reviewed.

Important:

ARCHIVE does NOT mean delete.

ARCHIVE does NOT mean automatically remove.

ARCHIVE means review.

No automatic deletions.

No automatic removals.

No automatic archival actions.

Step 4

Present complete report for approval.

Step 5

Only after approval may migration planning continue.

Snapshot Rule

Products contain default operational values.

Examples:

* Default Cost
* Preferred Catalog
* Placement Template
* Product Metadata
* Product Attributes

When a Product is selected for:

* Quote
* Project
* Mockup
* Order

the Product Dataset supplies the initial defaults.

After the record is created:

The Quote, Project, Mockup, or Order owns its own values.

Changes to Product records do not retroactively modify historical records.

Historical records must never automatically change because a Product record changed later.

Example:

NL6210

Default Cost:
$4.50

Quote #1001 created:

Product Cost:
$4.50

User edits Quote #1001:

Product Cost:
$4.85

Later:

NL6210 Default Cost changes to $5.10

Result:

Quote #1001 remains $4.85

Future quotes use $5.10.
