CRM Consolidation Phase — Remove Hub Management and Merge Into Organization Details

STOP.

Do not audit.

Do not roadmap.

Implement.

The current Hub Management page has become a duplicate management surface that competes with Organization Details.

This has repeatedly caused synchronization issues between:

* Contacts
* Hub Members
* Org Admins
* Hub Access
* Readiness

The architecture must be simplified.

Organization Details is now the single source of truth.

---

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Retire Hub Management as a management interface.

Organization Details becomes the only place where Client Hub configuration is managed.

CRM is the source of truth.

Organization
→ Contacts
→ Client Hub

Everything derives from that.

The following data must originate from Organization Details:

* Contacts
* Roles

* Org Admin
* Hub Access
* Client Hub Status
* Branding
* Hub URL
* Hub Readiness

No secondary datasets.

No duplicate member lists.

No duplicate admin lists.
