That plan is good, but it's still missing several implementation details that will save you from ending up with a "looks right but behaves wrong" result. I'd have Replit amend the plan before it starts writing code.

Add these implementation requirements
1. Preserve the data model

Do not convert every Product into its own Line Item.

The architecture should become:

Quote
 ├── Line Item (Design)
 │     ├── Design Details
 │     ├── Product #1
 │     ├── Product #2
 │     ├── Product #3
 │     ├── Calculator
 │     ├── Costs
 │     └── Subtotal
 │
 ├── Line Item
 │     ├── Product #1
 │     └── Product #2

A Line Item represents a design, not a garment.

2. Product synchronization

Every Product inside a Line Item must stay synchronized with the rest of the workspace.

Selecting or changing:

Product Type
Product Style
Product Color

must automatically update:

available colors
available sizes
product image (if available)
Product Source
Mockup Designer default garment

No duplicate product state should exist.

There should be a single source of truth.
