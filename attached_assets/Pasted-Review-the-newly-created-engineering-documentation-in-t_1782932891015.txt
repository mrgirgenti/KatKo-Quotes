/docs/BUSINESS_RULES.md

Examples include (but are not limited to):

----------------------------------------------------------
QUOTES

• Quote Builder defines the job.
• Quotes contain one or more Line Items.
• Line Items contain one or more Products.
• Line Item Costs use the five pricing buckets.

----------------------------------------------------------
PRICING

• Product Pricing owns apparel size upcharges.
• Service Styles determine quantity mode.
• Promotional products use flat quantities.
• Apparel uses size aggregation.
• Production Costs are directly related to production.
• Other Charges are indirect costs associated with completing the order.
• Quote Summary always rolls up all Line Items.

----------------------------------------------------------
MOCKUPS

• Mockup Builder consumes Quote Builder data.
• Mockups never own quote pricing.
• Locations defined in Quote Builder become artwork locations.
