CONTACTS ARCHITECTURE AUDIT & RESTRUCTURE

OBJECTIVE

The current Contacts page appears to be duplicating information already shown in Organizations.

Before implementing changes, audit the current architecture and then restructure Contacts into a true people directory.

==================================================
CURRENT CONCERN
==================================================

Organizations should represent:

Companies
Schools
Churches
Teams
Nonprofits
Businesses

Examples:

Battlestar Coffee Co
Grace AZ
Riverside High School

Contacts should represent:

Actual people

Examples:

Josh Girgenti
Jane Doe
Karla Widger
Mike Smith

Currently the Contacts page appears to be organization-centric instead of person-centric.

This creates duplication between Organizations and Contacts.

==================================================
STEP 1 — AUDIT CURRENT CONTACTS ARCHITECTURE
==================================================

Verify:

1. What data source powers Organizations?
2. What data source powers Contacts?
3. Are Contacts actually individual people?
4. Are Contacts currently organization records?
5. Is there already a relationship between:
   Organization → Contacts?
6. Are Client Hub users stored separately?
7. Are portal users already linked to contacts?
8. Are there duplicate records being created?

Provide findings before making assumptions.

==================================================
STEP 2 — DEFINE CONTACTS AS PEOPLE
==================================================

Contacts should become the master directory of every person in the ecosystem.

Examples:

Josh Girgenti
Jane Doe
Karla Widger
Mike Smith

A Contact belongs to an Organization.

Example:

Josh Girgenti
Organization: Battlestar Coffee Co

Karla Widger
Organization: Grace AZ

==================================================
STEP 3 — CLIENT HUB USERS
==================================================

Do NOT create a separate people database.

Client Hub users should be treated as Contacts.

A Contact may or may not have Hub access.

Examples:

Josh Girgenti
Hub Access: Active

Jane Smith
Hub Access: Invited

Mike Jones
Hub Access: No Access

All remain Contacts.

==================================================
STEP 4 — CONTACTS PAGE REDESIGN
==================================================

Convert Contacts into a people directory.

Columns / fields should include:

Name

Organization

Title / Role

Email

Phone

Hub Status

Last Login

Status

==================================================
HUB STATUS FIELD
==================================================

Supported values:

No Access

Invited

Active

Disabled

This should allow users to quickly identify portal users.

==================================================
FILTERS
==================================================

Add filters:

All Contacts

Portal Users

Non-Portal Users

Active Contacts

Inactive Contacts

By Organization

By Role

==================================================
METRICS ROW
==================================================

Replace organization-style metrics with people metrics.

Examples:

Total Contacts

Portal Users

Invited

Organizations Represented

==================================================
CLIENT HUB RELATIONSHIP
==================================================

The Client Hub page remains.

However:

Client Hubs become an administrative management view.

Example:

Battlestar Coffee
3 Users

Grace AZ
1 User

Riverside High School
5 Users

This page manages hubs.

The Contacts page manages people.

==================================================
ORGANIZATION RECORDS
==================================================

Organizations continue to display:

Contacts section

Client Hub section

Projects

Quotes

Activity

Notes

Media

No changes needed here.

==================================================
DATA MODEL REQUIREMENT
==================================================

Verify and maintain:

Organization
    ↓
Contacts (many)

Contact
    ↓
Optional Client Hub Access

Avoid creating duplicate people records.

A single Contact should be the source of truth.
