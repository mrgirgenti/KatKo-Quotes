ACTION CENTER — FULL IMPLEMENTATION SPRINT

STOP.

Do not audit.

Do not roadmap.

Implement.

The purpose of Action Center is to become the operational command center of Katalyst OS.

This is NOT:

- A notification center
- An activity log
- A messaging center
- An inbox

It is:

A prioritized work queue for items that require human attention.

Action Center should answer one question:

"What requires action right now?"

If no action is required, it does not belong in Action Center.
