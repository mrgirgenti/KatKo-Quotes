This iteration is very close. The overall architecture is now correct and the Quote Builder is finally beginning to feel like a production workspace instead of a collection of unrelated forms.

From this point forward, focus ONLY on refinement and polish. Do not redesign the layout or introduce new concepts. The approved mockup remains the source of truth.

IMPORTANT

If any existing implementation conflicts with the approved design or architecture, delete and replace it. Do not preserve legacy UI for backward compatibility.

==================================================
1. MERGE THE PRICING PANELS
==================================================

Merge the current "Line Item Costs" and "Line Item Subtotal" into a single pricing panel.

Keep the existing black "Line Item Costs" header.

Retain the existing editable fields at the top:

• Product
• Service
• Fees
• Markup

Then add a simple divider beneath those fields.

Below the divider, create a "Line Item Subtotal" subsection.

Remove the large orange "Line Item Subtotal" section entirely.

The entire pricing area for a line item should exist as one compact card aligned beside the production calculator, matching the proportions shown in the approved mockup.

This will:

• reduce vertical space
• improve readability
• keep all pricing for the line item together
• visually connect pricing to the production calculator

The pricing panel should feel like ONE cohesive component instead of two stacked widgets.

==================================================

==================================================

The Quote Summary sidebar is working well.

Keep the new "Line Item Subtotals" section.

Maintain this section.
