* Enabling Client Hub Access on Contacts does not properly create access
* Hub Management does not correctly pull Organization Contacts
* Org Admin assignment is disconnected
* Team Members section is disconnected

Audit:

Organization
↓
Contacts
↓
Hub Settings
↓
Hub Activation
↓
Org Admin
↓
Team Members
↓
Client Hub Access

Identify:

* Where activation state is stored
* Why activation state is not persisting
* Why readiness updates without activation
* Why contacts are not appearing
* Why Hub access settings are not synchronizing

Fix root causes.

No temporary workarounds.
